The Building Blocks

Macronutrients: build, fuel, support.

Macronutrients are the nutrients your body needs in large amounts to fuel performance, support growth, and maintain daily function. There's no single "perfect" nutrient — it's the balance that creates results.

Protein

Build. Repair. Recover.
  • Muscle repair and growth
  • Supports immune & hormone function
  • Preserves lean muscle during fat loss
  • Provides lasting satiety
Best sources: chicken, turkey, lean beef, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, tofu, lentils, protein powder.

Carbohydrates

Fuel. Perform. Endure.
  • Primary fuel for high-intensity activity
  • Stored in muscles and liver
  • Improves endurance, delays fatigue
  • Supports brain function
Best sources: oats, rice, whole grain pasta, quinoa, whole grain bread, beans, lentils, fruit.

Fats

Support. Protect. Balance.
  • Supports hormone production
  • Aids absorption of vitamins A, D, E, K
  • Provides long-lasting energy
  • Supports brain and heart health
Best sources: avocados, nuts, seeds, olive oil, nut butters, fatty fish, eggs.

Hydration & Electrolytes

The foundation of performance.

Water is essential for every function in your body. When you train, sweat, and go about your day, you lose fluid and electrolytes — both must be replaced to perform and recover at your best.

The Key Electrolytes
  • Sodium — maintains fluid balance and supports nerve function.
  • Potassium — supports muscle function and heart rhythm.
  • Magnesium — aids muscle relaxation and reduces cramping.
  • Calcium — important for muscle contraction and bone health.
  • Chloride — helps maintain fluid balance and digestion.
Hydration Strategy
  • BeforeDrink 16–24 oz of water 2–3 hours before activity, then another 8–12 oz 10–20 minutes before you start.
  • DuringDrink 7–10 oz every 10–20 minutes and include electrolytes for longer sessions.
  • AfterRefuel within 30–60 minutes to replace fluid and electrolytes lost in sweat.
