Life After College:
Your Next Chapter. Your Biggest Opportunity.

Your college career is a chapter, not the whole book. The preparation you do today shapes the opportunities you'll have tomorrow. The goal isn't just to prepare for the next game — it's to prepare for the rest of your life.

The Transition Is Real

You're not alone. You don't have to figure it out alone.

Leaving the structure, identity, and daily routine of college athletics is one of the biggest transitions of your life.

1

Identity Shift

Redefine who you are beyond your sport and what drives you.

2

Uncertainty

Navigate uncertainty with a clear plan and support.

3

Mental Adjustment

Adjust to new routines, responsibilities, and expectations.

4

New Opportunities

Embrace new opportunities and possibilities.

Keys to a Successful Transition
  • Plan Ahead — preparation today creates confidence for tomorrow
  • Invest in Yourself — keep learning, growing, and building your skillset
  • Build Your Network — relationships open doors, stay connected
  • Stay Adaptable — flexibility helps you thrive in any environment
  • Lead Your Life — take ownership of your future and your decisions
Your Career Won't Last Forever
  • Athletic careers can end due to age, performance, or injury
  • Life after sports can be even more demanding — with the right plan
  • Your future is in your hands, starting now
  • Resilience is your greatest asset when the unexpected happens
Potential Pathways After College

Continue your career. Apply your discipline.

Professional Sports

Pro leagues, overseas opportunities, independent leagues, tryout pathways.

Careers & Employment

Corporate careers, sales & business development, government & nonprofits.

Graduate School

MBA & business, sports management, education, health professions.

Entrepreneurship

E-commerce, coaching & training, digital content, consulting.

Coaching & Mentoring

Coaching, training, youth programs, mentorship.

Service & Impact

Community leadership, advocacy, international work, nonprofit leadership.

Beyond Sports

Creative arts, technology, real estate, skilled trades.

Aiming to Go Pro?

Research leagues, work with trusted advisors, build your brand, and always have a backup plan.

Injuries Can Happen. Be Prepared.
  • Have a support system in place
  • Stay proactive with rehab and recovery
  • Understand your insurance and healthcare options
  • Keep developing skills on and off the field
  • Have a plan for different outcomes
The Power of Saving & Money Management
  • Live below your means
  • Save consistently and invest wisely
  • Avoid lifestyle inflation
  • Plan ahead for taxes
  • Build multiple income streams
Your Plan

The 30/60/90 day plan after college.

30 Days
Focus: Stabilize
  • Set up your financial accounts
  • Create a budget
  • Update your resume & LinkedIn
  • Explore career options
60 Days
Focus: Build Momentum
  • Attend networking events
  • Build new relationships
  • Apply to jobs & opportunities
  • Develop new skills
90 Days
Focus: Grow & Commit
  • Secure interviews & offers
  • Set long-term goals (6–12 months)
  • Keep developing professionally
  • Give back & mentor others
