Why Student-Athletes Transfer

Transferring can open new doors academically, athletically, and personally. Here's what typically drives the decision:

Academic Fit

Find the right academic environment, major opportunities, and support systems.

Athletic Opportunity

Increase playing time, compete at a different level, or find a program that better fits your goals.

Culture & Environment

Seek a campus culture, coaching staff, and team environment that aligns with your values.

Coaching Changes

New coaches or staff changes can impact your role and future at the institution.

Personal Growth

A new start can provide the chance to grow, reset, and reach your full potential.

The Transfer Process: Step-by-Step

  • 1
    Evaluate your reasons — be honest about why you want to transfer and make sure it aligns with your long-term goals.
  • 2
    Know the rules — understand NCAA transfer rules, eligibility requirements, and your conference policies.
  • 3
    Notify your coach & advisors — communicate professionally with your coaching staff and academic advisors.
  • 4
    Enter the transfer portal — create your profile to connect with other schools.
  • 5
    Research & connect — identify schools that fit academically, athletically, and culturally, and reach out to coaches.
  • 6
    Visit & evaluate — visit campuses, talk with players, and evaluate if it's the right fit.
  • 7
    Make your decision — choose the school that gives you the best opportunity to succeed on and off the field.
NCAA Transfer Eligibility

Eligibility depends on several factors, including division level, whether you've used a season of competition, academic progress, and time in residence at your current school.

  • One-Time Transfer Exception (D1): may be immediately eligible if you haven't used a season of competition
  • Season of Competition: used when you compete in more than 30% of your team's contests
  • Residence Requirements: may need a year in residence before becoming eligible
  • Academic Requirements: must be in good academic standing and making progress toward your degree
  • Always check with the NCAA Eligibility Center and your compliance office before deciding
What Coaches Look For
  • Strong academic record
  • Positive leadership and character
  • Immediate or future athletic impact
  • Coachability and work ethic
  • Culture fit with the team
  • A clear reason for transferring

Transfer Tips for Success

  • Be transparent and honest in your communication
  • Keep your grades a top priority
  • Have a highlight video and updated athletic profile
  • Respond to coaches quickly and professionally
  • Use your support system — family, mentors, and advisors
  • Be patient and trust the process

Timeline: the best time to enter the portal is typically after your season ends. The most active recruiting periods are Spring and Summer, though fall transfers are possible.

Things to Consider Before Transferring

Before you make the move, sit with these questions honestly:

  • Will this move help me grow academically and athletically?
  • Can I afford the cost of attendance at my new school?
  • Do I connect with the coaching staff, team, and campus culture?
  • Will this school help me achieve my long-term goals?
  • Have I taken time to reflect and make the right decision for me?

Transfer Resources & Support

You don't have to navigate this alone — lean on these resources along the way:

NCAA Transfer Portal

Create your profile and connect directly with programs looking for your skill set.

NCAA Eligibility Center

Confirm your eligibility status before you commit to a decision.

Speak With Your Compliance Office

Get clarity on rules, timelines, and requirements specific to your situation.

Academic Advisor

Make sure credits transfer and your degree progress stays on track.

Family & Mentors

Lean on the people who know you best to help you think it through.
