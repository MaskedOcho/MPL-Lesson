The Training Pillars

Practice with intention.

The difference between players who improve quickly and those who plateau is almost always how they practice — not how much.

Technical Reps

Isolated, repeatable drills that build clean mechanics before adding speed or pressure.

Live-Ball Drilling

Fed and rally-based drills that bridge the gap between technique and real point play.

Point Play & Situations

Practice the specific situations that show up in real matches, not just clean rallies.

Physical Conditioning

Footwork, speed, and endurance work integrated directly into on-court sessions.

Recovery Between Sessions

Give your body and your strokes time to adapt — improvement happens between sessions too.

Reflection & Notes

Track what you worked on and what actually improved to guide your next session.

Structure Your Sessions

A simple framework for every practice.

1

Warm-Up & Activation

Get your body and strokes ready with movement prep and easy rallying before adding intensity.

2

Skill Block

Focused, purposeful drilling on one or two specific things you're actively trying to improve.

3

Live Play

Apply what you worked on in point play or match situations so it transfers to real games.
